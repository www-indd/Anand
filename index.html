Common Questions

Answers to Your FAQ

Does root canal treatment hurt? +
No. Our advanced painless anesthesia techniques and single-visit precision endodontics make RCT comfortable. Most patients report it's less painful than having a cavity filled. Dr. Rana specializes in making the experience stress-free.
How long do dental implants last? +
With proper care, dental implants can last 25+ years or even a lifetime. They're a permanent solution to missing teeth. Our 15+ years of implant experience ensures lasting results.
  • Titanium construction ensures durability
  • Success rate over 99% with proper care
  • Regular checkups extend longevity
Do you offer same-day dental treatment? +
Yes! We specialize in same-day treatments including single-visit root canals, emergency extractions, and temporary restorations. Complex cases may require 2-3 visits.
What are your treatment costs? +
Costs vary by treatment. We offer transparent pricing, EMI options, and cashless payments. Call us for a personalized quote: +91 99049 75842
  • Flexible payment plans available
  • Insurance claims assistance
  • No hidden charges
Do you treat children? +
Absolutely! Our pediatric dentistry program is designed to make children comfortable. We focus on preventive care, gentle techniques, and building lifelong oral health habits.
How is implant treatment different from a bridge? +
Implants are permanent, replace the tooth root, and preserve jawbone. Bridges are cheaper but require cutting adjacent teeth and don't prevent bone loss. Implants are the modern gold standard for lasting results.
What's your infection control & sterilization protocol? +
We follow strict WHO protocols. Every instrument is steam-sterilized, we use disposable gloves and masks, and maintain a clinically sterile environment. Your safety is our top priority.
Can I get a second opinion from Dr. Rana? +
Yes. Many patients come for a second opinion. Dr. Rana will review your X-rays and previous treatment plans. Book a consultation: +91 99049 75842. We're here to help you make the best decision for your smile.
